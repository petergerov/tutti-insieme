Simple by design

Open it. Everyone’s in.

There is nothing to set up. Every device listens along on its own, and whoever picks a song plays it.

01
◎

Join the circle

Open the app and it finds the others nearby. Several groups in one place? Each one is its own circle — pick yours once, it remembers.

02
▶

Pick a song

From your music or anyone else’s. The device the song is on plays it; the others become listeners, automatically.

03
≋

Hear it together

Every phone plays the same moment. Bluetooth speakers and headphones are taken into account, and a slider fixes the rest by ear.

Made for groups

Nobody owns the aux.

Most music apps have one person with the phone and everyone else asking. Tutti Insieme gives the whole group the remote.

▤

One catalogue for everyone

Your folders first, then the music of every device nearby — browsed and searched as one.

≡

A shared queue

Anyone adds songs or whole albums, reorders, removes. A checkmark shows what is queued, a speaker shows what plays.

⇄

Anyone can take over

Pick a song and your device plays it. The previous host simply listens along — no menus, no hand-over dance.

◇

Circles, open or private

Living room and kitchen, two parties on one Wi‑Fi: each circle only sees its own music and queue. A private circle needs a code — without it, nobody listens in.

↻

Keeps playing

The phone that played left the party? The next device with a song in the queue picks up, and everyone follows.

⌁

No Wi‑Fi needed

Same Wi‑Fi, a Personal Hotspot, or straight from device to device — at the beach, in the car, at the campsite.

Your music

The songs you own. On every phone.

Tutti Insieme plays music that lives on your devices. That is what makes perfect sync possible — and it is honest about what it cannot do.

Folders from Files and iCloud Drive

MP3, AAC/M4A, WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC. Browse by folder, album, artist — in track order, from the tags.

Your Music app library

Purchased, synced and imported songs on the device join the catalogue. New ones show up without a restart.

Playlists

Make playlists from your music. The others see them and can play them — on your device.

Not the subscription

Streaming subscriptions like Apple Music or Spotify are copy-protected and cannot be passed on. Apple Music subscription songs in your library show greyed out, so nobody wonders where they went.
