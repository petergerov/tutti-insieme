Sound quality & listeners

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The playing device sends every listener its own stream. The more listeners, the more the connection has to carry. So Tutti picks, for each listener, the best quality the connection has room for. There is nothing to set.

An MP3 or AAC file goes out as it is whenever it fits: the smallest stream, and nothing is compressed twice.

These are the limits Tutti plans with, and they leave room for whatever else uses the network. How many devices really play smoothly also depends on the router, the signal and how busy the Wi‑Fi is. A Personal Hotspot itself only lets a limited number of devices join, depending on the iPhone and the carrier.

Up to three devices listen together for free, the playing one included. Tutti Insieme Pro removes the limit.

Hi-res files (88.2 and 96 kHz)

Hi-res songs always stay lossless and keep their sample rate. At 96 kHz that is about 2 Mbit/s per listener, so lossless fits for up to 9 listeners on the same Wi‑Fi, 4 on a Personal Hotspot and 2 directly. With more listeners they still get lossless, which can stutter on a weak connection. For a big circle, a version of the song at 44.1 or 48 kHz is the safer choice.

Tips for many listeners

  • The same Wi‑Fi works best. Directly without a router works too, but it carries the least.
  • A Mac connected to the router by cable takes half the load off the Wi‑Fi when it plays.
  • A device with weak signal slows everyone down. Bring it closer to the router.
  • MP3 and AAC files need the least bandwidth and are passed on without any loss.

See what each device gets

On the playing device, the player shows the song's source, for example “Source: FLAC · 96 kHz · 24 Bit”. Under Listening, each device shows what it receives, for example “ALAC · 44.1 kHz · 16 Bit” or “AAC · 44.1 kHz · 256 kbit/s”. Listeners see the same under “Receiving” in their player.
