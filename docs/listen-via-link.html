Listen via Link

Updated: November 24, 2026 · coming with Tutti Insieme 1.0.1

Anyone without Tutti listens along anyway: with an Android phone, a Windows or macOS computer, or an iPhone without the app. Nothing to install, no account. Every iPhone or iPad with Tutti offers a small web page on your Wi‑Fi. Open the link or scan the QR code, pick your circle, listen in sync with everyone else.

How it works

  1. On an iPhone or iPad with Tutti, tap Listen via Link under Circle. It shows an address like http://192.168.1.23:8737 and a QR code. The button appears when the device is on a network by Wi‑Fi or cable.
  2. On the other device, on the same Wi‑Fi, scan the QR code with the camera or type the address into a browser such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.
  3. Pick the circle: the page asks on the first visit, even when there is only one. Next time the phone returns to the same circle on its own.
  4. Tap Start listening. The music comes from the device playing in the circle and follows on its own when another one takes over.

What the browser can do

  • Player: title, the song’s format (“Source”) and what the browser receives (“Receiving”), back, play/pause and next for everyone in the circle.
  • Queue: see what plays and what comes next; drag to reorder, play songs now, move them up or remove them.
  • Settings: change the circle, or leave it to stop listening and pick again; the device’s name, and “Sound too early / too late” for Bluetooth headphones.

Back works as in an app: it closes a menu first, then goes from the queue or the settings to the player, and keeps listening. Requesting new songs from the catalogue works in the app only, for now.

Which connection works

The browser needs a network it shares with the device that plays.

ConnectionIn the browser
Same Wi‑FiYes, best
Personal Hotspot of the iPhone that playsNo: “Listen via Link” is offered only on Wi‑Fi or by cable
The Android phone’s hotspotYes: join the Android phone’s hotspot with the iPhone
Direct, without Wi‑Fi (like AirDrop)No, that works only between Apple devices with the app
Wi‑Fi AwareNo, browsers cannot use it

iPhone without the app

Without Tutti installed, an iPhone can open the page in Safari as well, on the same Wi‑Fi. With the silent switch on, Safari may stay quiet. The app works better on an iPhone, also with the screen locked.

Tips

  • If the music stops when the phone locks, keep the screen on.
  • If the phone sounds late, for example with Bluetooth headphones, slide “Sound too early / too late” to the right. +100 to +250 ms often fits.
  • Private circles work too: tap the circle with the lock and enter its code. Or scan the QR code under “Listen via Link” on a device of the circle, or open one of the links there; they hold the code. The page then encrypts everything with the code, like the app.
  • A browser counts as a listener. Up to three devices listen together for free, the playing one included; Tutti Insieme Pro lifts the limit.
